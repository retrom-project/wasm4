# FULL STEAM AHEAD TO THE RESCUE

Help, help! There was an accident at the construction of this railroad. You're
a locomotive driver, right? We need you to hop on this locomotive and drive to
the end of this track to get a rescue time to the accident site. I guess you 
know how to drive a locomotive but here is a small recap:

* With the `c` button coal is added to the oven, which increase the pressure tank,
  you need to keep shoveling coal in the oven to subsequently increase the pressure.
  The tank loose pressure on its own, so you need check for the pressure, and
  shovel more coals into the oven. If the pressure is too low for a certain speed
  the locomotive will slow down
* With the `v` button you can reduce the pressure in the tank, this is important
  when you reach the accident site, so that you can stop the acceleration
  or reduce over-pressure in the tank. If the tank has over-pressure for too long
  it will explode, and you won't reach the accident site. A lamp with a countdown
  indicates that the tank has too much pressure.
* The tank above the buttons indicated how much pressure is in the tank, when
  reaching the accident site you need to reduce the pressure to the 20 %, or 
  otherwise the locomotive will not stop moving when you release the break
* The `brake` button brakes the locomotive, this is necessary when you reach the
  accident site. You need to stop the locomotive when you reach the end of the 
  track.

You need to hurry up, you only have `120` seconds too reach the accident site in
`1600` m distance. You need to reach it before the countdown runs out. Try to get
as close to the accident site as possible so that the rescue team can reach the 
casualties faster. Don't overshoot or you will crash, killing the team and
yourself in the process. If you reach the accident site fast and as close as
possible you will get the best `score`.

Godspeed, and FULL STEAM AHEAD TO THE RESCUE!
